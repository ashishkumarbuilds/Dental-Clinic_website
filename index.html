Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about booking, patient privacy, and our clinic policies.

How do I get my Unique Patient ID?
Your Unique Patient ID (e.g., DC1028) is generated automatically as soon as you confirm an appointment on our booking page or register with clinic reception. It is displayed on your printable digital booking slip and allows you to log in to access your personal dental timeline.
Is my medical information private from other patients?
Yes, strictly. DentaCare implements enterprise role-based security. Full operatory management, clinical diagnosis logs, and all patient records are restricted to licensed clinic staff. Patients can only view their own confidential dental history using their Unique Patient ID.
Do you handle emergency dental walk-ins?
Absolutely. We reserve daily emergency buffer slots across all three operatories for severe tooth pain, fractured teeth, or trauma. You can book the next available slot online or call reception directly at +91 98765 43210 for prompt care.
What payment methods and insurance do you accept?
We accept all UPI applications (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit/debit cards, net banking, and zero-interest EMI plans for aligners and implants. We also provide itemized medical invoices with procedure codes for corporate dental health insurance claims.
How does the automated follow-up reminder work?
After any restorative or surgical procedure, our system logs your recommended healing evaluation date. You can track your upcoming recall date in your patient portal, and reception will follow up to ensure your recovery is smooth.
