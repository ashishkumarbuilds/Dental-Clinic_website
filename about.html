About DentaCare

Modern Dentistry, Organized Simply.

A professional academic prototype designed to unify the end-to-end dental patient journey.

Our Mission

DentaCare eliminates fragmented records in outpatient dental clinics by bringing demographics, scheduled visits, procedural timelines, and recalls into a single connected interface.


The layout is streamlined so receptionists, dental nurses, and specialist surgeons can access vital clinical notes with minimal friction.

Core Clinical Values

✓ Patient-Centric Profiles: Medical history, allergies, and contact data preserved centrally.

✓ Procedure Transparency: Multi-visit endodontics and orthodontics tracked in stages.

✓ Frictionless Scheduling: Real-time slot management preventing chair double-booking.

✓ Systematic Follow-Ups: Automated recall records reducing post-op complication rates.
