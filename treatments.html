Standard Dental Care Timeline Stages

1. Consultation & Screening

Chief patient complaint recorded, intraoral examination and preliminary X-rays taken.

2. Clinical Diagnosis & Plan

Definitive condition diagnosed, treatment options, estimates, and consent formulated.

3. Clinical Procedure

Tooth preparation, cavity restoration, endodontic therapy, or aligner placement initiated.

4. Post-Treatment Follow-up

Bite evaluation, healing validation, oral hygiene reinforcement, and maintenance schedule.
